Expense Splitter

Split bills with roommates and friends. It works out who owes whom, suggests a short list of payments to settle everything, and opens your UPI app to pay. Works offline; data stays on the phone.

Download the APK (1.4 MB)

Android 8.0 or newer. Signed release build that asks for no permissions: no internet, contacts or storage. No ads.

Installing it

  1. Tap Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser (Settings, then "Install unknown apps").
  3. Open Expense Splitter. It starts with a sample group, "Flat 304", so you can try it straight away.

What's inside

  • Kotlin, Jetpack Compose (Material 3), Room database, Navigation, ViewModel with Flow
  • Amounts stored in paise as whole numbers, so ₹100 split three ways is ₹33.34 + ₹33.33 + ₹33.33, never ₹99.99
  • Settle-up uses a greedy algorithm: the person who owes most pays the person owed most, which needs at most (people minus 1) payments. Short, though not always the shortest possible.
  • "Pay via UPI" builds a upi://pay link with the amount filled in; "Mark as paid" records the settlement
  • Unit tests for splitting, balances and settle-up
